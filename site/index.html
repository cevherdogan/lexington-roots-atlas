Connections are questions, not assumptions

Lexington, VA, Lexington, MA and Lexington Avenue, NYC share a name; their precise naming histories must be investigated independently. The Appalachian Trail belongs to the surrounding mountain region; Lexington is not itself an Appalachian Trail trailhead.
